# Stocks

**Stock**
- An ==equity security==. A unit of ownership in a corporation.
- Buying a share makes you a partial owner of that company.
**Common Stock**
- Grants voting rights (elect board of directors, approve major decisions).
- Dividends are not guaranteed -- paid at the board's discretion.
- Last in line during liquidation -- bondholders and preferred shareholders are paid first.
**Preferred Stock**
- No voting rights (typically).
- Fixed dividends -- paid before common stock dividends.
- Higher claim on assets during liquidation than common stock.
- A ==hybrid security== -- behaves more like a [[bonds|bond]] than a stock.
**Market Capitalization**
- Total value of a company's outstanding shares.
- Market cap = share price x total shares outstanding.
