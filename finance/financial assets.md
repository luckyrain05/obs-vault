# Market Types

**Primary Market**

- Where securities are created and sold for the first time.
- Companies issue new stocks or bonds directly to investors.
- An ==IPO (Initial Public Offering)== is a company's first sale of stock to the public.
- Money from the sale goes to the issuing company.

**Secondary Market**

- Where investors trade previously issued securities with each other.
- The issuing company receives no money from these trades.
- Stock exchanges (NYSE, NASDAQ) are secondary markets.

# Securities

- ==Securities== are marketable financial instruments that holds monetary value. 
- There are thousands realistically, modern markets are extremely complex. 
- Below are a few important types.

**Equity Security**

- Represents ownership in a [[business entities#Corporation|corporation]].
- Returns come from price appreciation and dividends.

**Debt Security**

- Represents a loan made by the investor to an issuer.
- Returns come from [[interest rates|interest]] payments and repayment of principal.

**Hybrid Security**

- Has characteristics of both debt and equity (convertible bonds, preferred stock).

# ETFs

- A fund that holds a basket of assets (stocks, bonds, commodities) and trades on an exchange like any other financial instrument.
